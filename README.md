# Bleary

**A migraine diary for iPhone.** Log an attack in one tap and see how barometric pressure and weather affect your migraines.

[**Download on the App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6785338448) · [Website](https://thomascodes123.github.io/Bleary/)

## What it does

- **One-tap logging.** Start a migraine in one tap and fill in details (symptoms, triggers, medication) now or later.
- **Weather and pressure risk.** A daily migraine-risk forecast based on barometric pressure and weather.
- **Live Activity.** An ongoing attack stays on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island with a timer.
- **Patterns.** Insights into frequency, triggers and medication over time.
- **Doctor report.** Export a PDF summary to take to an appointment.
- **Private by design.** No own backend: data stays on the device and in the user's iCloud.
- Available in Spanish, English, French, German and Portuguese.

## Built with

SwiftUI · SwiftData + CloudKit · WidgetKit / ActivityKit · WeatherKit · HealthKit · RevenueCat

Developed with AI-assisted coding (Claude Code).
